Parc Fermé

Eight screens showing one race weekend, from signing in to the leaderboard after the race. The drivers, team colours and Singapore GP schedule are real OpenF1 data. The players, picks and results are example data. See also the desktop versions.

asphalt #0E0F12 ink #ECEEF1 best of weekend open / gained lock Barlow Condensed · IBM Plex Sans · JetBrains Mono
Why this is dark: it echoes an F1 timing screen. Purple and green mean what they mean on the pit wall, the best of the weekend and a gain.
Colour use: team colours only ever appear as a thin bar next to a driver, so 22 drivers don't turn into a rainbow.
Not shown yet: the light theme, and empty or error states such as no upcoming race or a sign-in link that has expired.
